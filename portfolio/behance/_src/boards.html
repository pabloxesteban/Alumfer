Corporate website: information architecture, interface design, front-end development, local search and editorial content.

Overview

Alumfer is a family-run manufacturer in the southern suburbs of Buenos Aires. It fabricates and installs custom aluminum windows, doors, shutters and enclosures in its own workshop, with no middlemen and no subcontractors.

The brief was a website with the credibility of a manufacturer: show finished work, explain product lines and glazing in plain language, and make a quote request effortless on any device.

Information architecture

Content was structured before any visual design, around four intents: finding a product, finding a local supplier, researching before buying, and asking for a price. Every path ends in the same place: a direct conversation with the workshop.

Brand system

The company’s symbol was kept intact. Around it, the site borrows the language of a technical drawing: dimension lines, numbered sections, an archive of finished work and monospaced labels, like the notes on a workshop plan.

Night blue and navy carry the brand, a single bright blue marks actions and key details, and warm paper sections give the photography room to breathe.
